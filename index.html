The Main Air-Conditioned Hall

Features a spacious pillarless design ensuring unobstructed viewing of the Mandap from every angle. Fitted with modern crystal chandelier illumination, acoustic wall finishes, and heavy-duty central AC units for seamless year-round celebrations.

  • Elevated Stage: Wide elevated stage ideal for wedding Mandaps, floral arches, and VIP sofa seating.
  • Modern Lighting: Integrated false ceiling with warm white spotlights and dynamic LED tracks.
  • Acoustics: Sound-treated architecture reducing echo for priest chants, speeches, and music.

Spacious dining facility configured for both traditional South Indian leaf service (Banthi) and modern buffet catering.
